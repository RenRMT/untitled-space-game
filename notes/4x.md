# The 4X game

A fourth page beside the game, the race and the idle game: `/4x/` (`4x/index.html`, code in
`src/4x/`). It takes the main game's play area (the same galaxy from the same seed, at the same
scale in world units) and turns it from a ship the player flies into a map the player commands:
units are picked and given orders, and fly them out on their own.

## What there is now

- **One map, three zooms.** A single camera in world units zooms without a break from the flight
  view (the main game's own zoom, `game/state.js` `view.base`: ships and stations in their Line art)
  out to the star system (orbits, bodies as dots and icons) and on out to the galaxy (its glow and
  every system's star). The header's Flight / System / Galaxy buttons (or 1 / 2 / 3) ease the camera
  to each; the wheel and pinch zoom round the pointer as in the idle game's map (`src/idle/map.js`).
  The camera eases the target's offset on screen and the zoom separately, so a dive from the galaxy
  into a ship lands on it. A thing whose orbit is too small to see folds into what it goes round, and
  the start system folds into its star once it's a few px across.
- **The start system:** the start's star, one planet (its first temperate one in the main game's
  galaxy), the station the main game built round that planet, and one ship, Pathfinder, parked
  round the station. No hazards. The other systems are their stars only, uncharted.
- **Orders.** Pick a ship and send it to a station, planet or moon, where it parks in orbit, or to
  a point in space, where it holds position. How the order is given is one of four prototypes to
  compare (`src/4x/movement.js`, `notes/4x-movement-research.md`): Move on the card then a place
  (`button`), tap a place then Send (`tap`), drag from the ship (`drag`), or hold the ship and draw
  a path (`path`). `?move=` picks one and the header's Move button cycles them. In every one a
  right-click or long press sends the picked ship. It flies at a courier's speed, slowing to arrive.
  The player's ships are always drawn, even where their host has folded away. Focus follows the
  picked thing at the flight zoom. Esc cancels an order being given, or drops the pick.
- **Building.** The header's Build button (or B) switches to building: an Outpost or a Starbase
  round a planet or moon (at most one station round each), and any station dismantled. How it's done
  is one of three prototypes to compare (`src/4x/building.js`, `notes/4x-building-research.md`):
  tap a body and choose on the card, with Undo (`menu`); pick a kind in the tray, tap a body for a
  ghost, then Build (`ghost`); drag a kind from the tray onto a body, or a station off to remove it
  (`drag`). `?build=` opens in build mode with one, and the scheme button cycles them while building.
  A new station takes 2 s to finish; ships parked round a dismantled one park round its body.
- **`?testbed`**: the main game's whole start system (every planet, moon and planet station) with
  three ships, to try the schemes on.

## Orbit rules (`src/4x/orbits.js`)

- The star is at the centre.
- One or more planets go round the star.
- None or more moons go round a planet.
- At most one station goes round a planet or a moon.
- An idle ship goes round a station, a planet or a moon, or holds the point in space it was sent to.
- Asteroids lie in belts (a band round the star with a radius and a width), never in fields in one place.
- Wrecks of ships and stations gather in graveyards at a planet's L4 or L5 Lagrange point: on its
  orbit, 60° ahead or behind, going round with it.

`Orbits4X.check(nodes)` lists every rule a layout breaks; the world refuses to start with a broken
one, and the tests (`tests/run.js`, "4x orbit rules") check each rule. Periods follow the main
game's (`world/orbits.js`, Kepler's 1.5 power).

## Layers

`src/4x/` imports `src/core`, `art`, `world` and `rules` and the UI kit, never `src/game`,
`src/flight` or the main game's panels (ESLint checks). The parts:

| File | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| `orbits.js` | the orbit rules and placing everything at a moment of the clock |
| `world.js` | the galaxy, the start system, the units, orders, building and the clock (runs in Node) |
| `view.js` | the map: camera, zoom bands, drawing, picking, input |
| `movement.js` | the ways of giving move orders, as prototypes to compare |
| `building.js` | the ways of building and dismantling stations, as prototypes to compare |
| `main.js` | the page: header, build mode and its tray, the card for the picked thing, keys, the frame loop |
| `4x.css` | the page's layout over the UI kit's Line style |
